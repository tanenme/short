## Sumber fakta tertulis (minimal 2 sumber tier-1)
1. **Journal of Fluid Mechanics** (Cambridge University Press, 2021) — Zhi-Ming Yuan et al., *"Wave-riding and wave-passing by ducklings in formation swimming"*
   - URL: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/waveriding-and-wavepassing-by-ducklings-in-formation-swimming/945633B700405A456A5706101B63846B
   - Fakta terverifikasi: Anak bebek di posisi tepat di belakang induk memanfaatkan *wave-riding* (mengurangi hambatan air hingga 158%, berubah menjadi gaya dorong maju) lalu meneruskan energi gelombang ke anak bebek berikutnya (*wave-passing*).
2. **Science (AAAS)** — *"Why do ducklings swim in a row?"*
   - URL: https://www.science.org/content/article/why-do-ducklings-swim-row
   - Fakta terverifikasi: Formasi satu baris adalah adaptasi hidrodinamika hemat energi agar anak bebek mampu mengikuti induknya melintasi perairan jauh tanpa cepat kelelahan.
3. **Physics World** — *"Ducklings surf in formation to conserve energy"*
   - URL: https://physicsworld.com/a/ducklings-surf-in-formation-to-conserve-energy/
